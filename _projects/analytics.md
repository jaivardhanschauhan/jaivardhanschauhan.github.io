This set of case studies shows how we tackled real pricing and customer analytics problems during my MBA coursework. Each project starts with a clear business question, moves through data cleaning and modeling, and ends with plain-spoken recommendations that managers can act on. Techniques include CLV estimation, demand forecasting, regression, and segmentation, all grounded in the micro-economic and statistical methods taught in MKTG 568 (Pricing Strategy and Analytics) and MKTG 562 (Customer Analytics). Our team was later recognized by the Professor as one of the course’s best performers for turning raw data into clear customer-value and retention decisions.
